Website Privacy Policy

We want you to explore our work with a clear understanding of how these websites handle information.

Effective: September 26, 2026

Privacy at a glance We use Google Analytics only with your permission. You can browse the site if you decline, and change your choice later.

1. Sites and operator

This policy covers the One Team. official website and portfolio site. Our app privacy policy covers our applications separately.

These sites are operated by Haruki Miura, an creator working as One Team. For questions or privacy requests, email oneteam.dot@gmail.com.

2. Analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve how we present our work. It may collect pages viewed, visit times, approximate location, and browser and device information, and use cookies such as distinguish visitors. We do not send names or other directly identifying details to Analytics. Advertising personalization is disabled.

Until you accept, and if you decline, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded and no Analytics measurement data is sent by this site. Your choice is stored in this browser's local storage. You can change it using “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings.

3. Other connections

GitHub Pages hosts these sites. Its infrastructure may process technical connection data such as IP addresses while delivering pages and images. Some pages connect to third-party services for fonts, libraries, videos, or embedded content. Those services handle data under their own policies.

See also the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Contact

If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond. We delete information when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

Contact: oneteam.dot@gmail.com

5. Updates

We may revise this policy when the sites or services change. Updates will be posted here.
