Singapore

Privacy Policy

This notice describes how One Team. handles personal data of users in Singapore under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (“PDPA”).

Effective: 25 July 2026 · Last updated: 25 July 2026

No advertising. Our apps do not display third-party advertising. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

1. Organisation and Data Protection Contact

One Team. is the organisation responsible for the personal data described here. Our designated data protection contact handles questions, complaints, withdrawal of consent, and access or correction requests at oneteam.dot@gmail.com.

2. Collection, purposes, and consent

We automatically collect app activity (including gameplay and feature interactions), device and technical data (including device model, operating system, language or locale, carrier, app version, app-instance or similar identifiers, IP address, and diagnostics), and—depending on platform and SDK settings—an advertising identifier. We also collect the email address and content you choose to provide when contacting us.

We use this data to provide, secure, troubleshoot, analyse, and improve the apps; prevent fraud or abuse; respond to requests; and comply with law. We collect, use, or disclose personal data with consent, deemed consent, or another basis permitted by the PDPA, and only for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate.

Providing analytics consent is voluntary where the app offers that choice. If you do not provide or later withdraw consent, optional analytics may stop and some non-core features may be affected. Withdrawal applies after we have had reasonable time to process it and does not affect prior lawful processing.

3. Disclosure and overseas transfers

We use Google Firebase as a data intermediary for app analytics and performance. Google and its affiliates may process data in Japan, the United States, and other countries where they operate. We use contractual or other safeguards intended to provide protection comparable to the PDPA. We may also disclose data when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or during a business reorganisation with appropriate safeguards.

We do not disclose personal data to advertising networks. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Accuracy, protection, and retention

We take reasonable steps to keep personal data accurate where it may affect you and use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or disposal.

Analytics event data is retained for no longer than 14 months unless a shorter period is configured. Contact records are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet legal or security obligations. We then delete the data or remove the means by which it can be associated with an individual when retention no longer serves a legal or business purpose.

5. Access, correction, and withdrawal

You may ask for access to personal data in our possession or control and information about how it was used or disclosed during the preceding year, subject to legal exceptions. You may also request correction or withdraw consent with reasonable notice.

Email oneteam.dot@gmail.com with “Singapore PDPA Request” in the subject line. Describe your request and provide enough information for us to verify your identity. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible and explain any applicable fee before processing an access request.

6. Children, incidents, and complaints

We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child who cannot validly consent under applicable law without the required authorisation of a parent or guardian. If you believe this has occurred, contact us so we can investigate and delete the data where required.

We assess suspected data breaches and notify the Personal Data Protection Commission and affected individuals where required. Please first send any concern to our data protection contact so that we can investigate and respond.

7. Changes

We may update this notice to reflect changes to the apps, our practices, or the law. We will provide an appropriate notice of material changes. This Singapore notice prevails over the Global Privacy Policy where required by the PDPA.
