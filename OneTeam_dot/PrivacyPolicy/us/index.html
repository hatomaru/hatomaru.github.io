2. Retention and international processing

Analytics event data is retained for no longer than 14 months unless a shorter period is configured. Contact records are retained only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet legal or security obligations. We then delete or deidentify data when reasonably practicable.

One Team. operates from Japan. Google, its affiliates, and other service providers may process data in Japan, the United States, and other countries where they operate. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

3. State privacy rights

Subject to applicability, verification, and legal exceptions, residents of certain states may request that we:

  • confirm whether we process their personal information and provide access to or a portable copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • delete personal information;
  • provide information about categories, sources, purposes, and recipients; or
  • honor an opt-out from sale, targeted advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, or qualifying profiling.

We do not currently conduct the sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or qualifying profiling described above. We do not discriminate against users for exercising privacy rights.

Email oneteam.dot@gmail.com with “U.S. Privacy Request” in the subject line. Include your state of residence, the right requested, and enough information for us to verify the request. An authorised agent may submit a request where state law permits; we may require proof of authorisation and direct identity verification.

If we deny your request, residents of states that provide an appeal right may appeal by replying to our decision with “Privacy Appeal” in the subject line. We will explain our decision and, where required, how to contact the appropriate state regulator.

4. California notice

California residents may have rights to know, access, delete, and correct personal information and to receive equal service and pricing when exercising those rights. Because we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law, we do not provide a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit.

California’s “Shine the Light” law permits certain requests about disclosures for third parties’ direct-marketing purposes. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing.

5. Children’s privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information online from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent where required by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act. Persistent identifiers, including IP addresses and device identifiers, may be personal information under COPPA.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 provided personal information, email oneteam.dot@gmail.com. We will investigate and delete the information where required. If an app is later directed to children or we knowingly collect from children, we will provide a COPPA-compliant parental notice and consent process before collection.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this notice to reflect changes to the apps, our practices, or the law and will provide an appropriate notice of material changes. Questions and complaints may be sent to oneteam.dot@gmail.com. This U.S. notice controls over the Global Privacy Policy where mandatory U.S. law requires.
