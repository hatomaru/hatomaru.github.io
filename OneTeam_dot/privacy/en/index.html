Website Privacy Policy

We want you to explore our work with a clear understanding of how these websites handle information.

Effective: September 26, 2026 / Updated: October 17, 2026

Privacy at a glance Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page visits without cookies. We use Google Analytics and its cookies only with your permission. You can change your choice later.

1. Sites and operator

This policy covers the One Team. official website and portfolio site. Our app privacy policy covers our applications separately.

These sites are operated by Haruki Miura, an creator working as One Team. For questions or privacy requests, email oneteam.dot@gmail.com.

2. Analytics and cookies

Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to improve this site, including before you choose or if you decline Google Analytics. It does not use analytics cookies or browser local storage to identify visitors. We view aggregate information such as page views and loading performance. Loading its script and sending measurement data involves connections to Cloudflare. This measurement operates independently of your Google Analytics choice.

Google Analytics 4

Only if you accept, we load Google Analytics 4 to collect information such as pages viewed, visit times, approximate location, and browser and device information. It uses analytics cookies to distinguish visitors. We do not send names or other directly identifying details to Analytics. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled. Before you choose or if you decline, this site's Google Analytics tag is not loaded and no measurement data is sent to Google Analytics.

Saving and changing your choice

Your Google Analytics choice is stored in this browser's local storage. You can change it using “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page. When you withdraw consent, we stop Google Analytics measurement, delete analytics cookies that this site can remove, and reload the page. Cloudflare Web Analytics continues measuring without cookies. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings.

3. Other connections

GitHub Pages hosts these sites. Its infrastructure may process technical connection data such as IP addresses while delivering pages and images. Some pages connect to third-party services for fonts, libraries, videos, or embedded content. Those services handle data under their own policies.

See also the Cloudflare Privacy Policy, Cloudflare Web Analytics overview, Google Privacy Policy, and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Contact

If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond. We delete information when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

Contact: oneteam.dot@gmail.com

5. Updates

We may revise this policy when the sites or services change. Updates will be posted here.
